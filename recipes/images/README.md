# Recipe photos

Drop a photo here named to match the recipe it belongs to, for example:

    tomato-salad.jpg

Then add this line to that recipe's frontmatter in `recipes/tomato-salad.md`:

    image: tomato-salad.jpg

That's it — the photo shows as a thumbnail on the recipe card and as a larger photo at the top of the recipe page. Recipes without a photo just show their category icon instead, so photos can be added gradually, one recipe at a time.
